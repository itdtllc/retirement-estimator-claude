# Privacy Policy: Retirement Estimator for Claude

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy covers the **Retirement Estimator for Claude** plugin by ITDT LLC: the
`retirement-estimator` and `social-security-claiming` skills and their scripts. The Retirement
Estimator app has its own privacy policy at https://itdtllc.com/privacy.html.

## What the plugin is

The plugin is a set of instructions and reference files for Claude, plus two small Python scripts
(`spend_down.py` and `ss_claiming.py`) that do arithmetic and read or write the plan file you're
working on. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and makes no network calls.

## What ITDT LLC collects

Nothing. ITDT LLC does not receive, collect, or store your conversations, the financial details you
give Claude, or the plan files Claude makes.

## Where your information goes

- **Your conversation with Claude** stays in your Claude account. Anthropic handles it under its
  privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and your Claude privacy settings.
- **The scripts** run where Claude runs them: in Claude's code execution environment on claude.ai, or
  on your own computer in Claude Code or Cowork. They only read the numbers Claude passes them and
  the plan file you're working on, and they send nothing anywhere.
- **Plan files** are plain JSON files that you download and open in Retirement Estimator. Once
  imported, your data is stored on your device under the app's privacy policy.

## Usage figures from Anthropic

When the plugin is listed in Anthropic's plugin directory, Anthropic gives publishers aggregate
figures, such as the number of installs and how often each skill runs. ITDT LLC uses them only to see
whether the plugin is being used and working. They do not include your conversations or plan data.

## Children

The plugin is not intended for people under 18.
