# Retirement Estimator for Claude

This skill teaches Claude how to set up your financial plan in **Retirement Estimator** (ITDT LLC):
a retirement plan, a budget for your spending, or any other plan.
Claude asks you questions one at a time, builds your plan as a file, and you import it into the app.
Then Claude helps you read the results and try "what if" questions.

## What you need

- **Retirement Estimator 1.7.0 or later** on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac (Apple silicon), from the App Store.
- The app's **Import/Export Data** in-app purchase, included in **Premium**. You don't need to buy it
  until the last step, when you import your plan.
- A Claude plan that supports skills and includes **Opus**, which builds complex plans most reliably.

## Claude app or claude.ai: 4 steps

1. **Download** the Retirement Estimator skill: itdtllc.com/data/RetirementEstimatorSkill.zip
2. **Add it to Claude:** in claude.ai or the Claude app (not your device's Settings app), tap your
   name or initials ▸ **Settings** ▸ **Capabilities** and check that **Cloud code execution and file
   creation** is on. Then under **Skills** tap **+ Add** ▸ **Upload skill**, and pick the zip.
3. **Ask Claude:** choose **Opus** in Claude's model menu, then ask "Help me set up my plan in
   Retirement Estimator." Answer its questions. You can change any value Claude suggests.
4. **Open the plan file** Claude gives you:
   - **iPhone or iPad:** tap **Download** on the file (ignore any panel with Copy or Load file). Go
     to your Home Screen, open the **Files** app ▸ **Downloads**, and tap the plan file.
   - **Mac:** click **Download**, then double-click the plan file in your **Downloads** folder.

   Retirement Estimator opens, checks the plan, and imports it. Then you'll see your graphs.

## Claude Code: 2 steps

1. Tell Claude Code: "Add the Retirement Estimator skill from itdtllc.com/data/RetirementEstimatorSkill.zip"
2. Start a new Claude Code session, switch to Opus (`/model opus`), and ask: "Help me set up my plan in Retirement Estimator."

## Your privacy

Claude works on your behalf, like a helper entering your plan for you. Your information is held in
two places:

- **Claude (Anthropic):** what you tell Claude, and the plan it creates, are stored by Anthropic
  under your Claude account and its privacy settings.
- **Retirement Estimator (your device):** when you import the plan, the app saves it on your device,
  the same as data you enter yourself. The app does not send it anywhere.

**ITDT LLC** receives none of it: not your conversations with Claude, not your plan, and not your
app data.

## Suggestions, not financial advice

Claude sets up the numbers you provide and shows what the app projects. It can suggest ideas to try,
such as retiring at a different age or saving a little more, and you can compare them in the app.
These are suggestions, not financial advice. For financial advice, consult a qualified financial
advisor.
