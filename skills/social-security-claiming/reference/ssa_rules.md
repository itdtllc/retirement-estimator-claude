# SSA rules used by this skill

These are the rules for the person's own retirement benefit, as SSA publishes them. Sources:
the regulations in 20 CFR 404.409 (full retirement age), 404.410 (reduction for claiming early) and
404.313 (delayed retirement credits), and ssa.gov's "Retirement Benefits: Benefits Planner" pages.

## Full retirement age

| Birth year | FRA |
|---|---|
| 1943–1954 | 66 |
| 1955 | 66 and 2 months |
| 1956 | 66 and 4 months |
| 1957 | 66 and 6 months |
| 1958 | 66 and 8 months |
| 1959 | 66 and 10 months |
| 1960 and later | 67 |

Someone born on January 1 uses the previous year's row, because SSA counts an age as reached the
day before the birthday.

## When an age begins

A person reaches an age the day before their birthday (20 CFR 404.102).
- **Full retirement age, 70, and any claiming age after 62** start in the month the age is reached
  (404.409: unreduced benefits "beginning with the month you attain" FRA; 404.313: delayed credits
  run to the month you attain 70). Born on the 1st: that's the month before the birthday month.
- **Age 62** needs the whole month (404.311(a)(2): before full retirement age, benefits start with
  the first month "throughout which" every requirement, including being 62, is met). Born on the
  1st or 2nd: the birthday month. Born on any other day: the month after, i.e. 62 and 1 month.

So for most people (born after the 2nd), the earliest claim is one month short of the full early
reduction: with FRA 67, 59 months early = 70.42% of the FRA amount, not 70%.

## Reduction for claiming early

- 5/9 of 1% for each of the first 36 months before FRA (20% for 36 months).
- 5/12 of 1% for each month beyond 36 (5% a year).

## Credit for claiming late (born 1943 or later)

- 2/3 of 1% for each month after FRA (8% a year), up to age 70. Nothing is gained after 70.
- SSA actually adds credits earned during a year in the following January (except at 70). The
  skill applies them from the start, which is the amount the person settles at.

## Rounding and payment

- The monthly benefit is rounded down to the whole dollar.
- The benefit for a month is paid in the following month.

## Not covered

Spousal and survivor benefits, the earnings test (benefits withheld if working before FRA),
Medicare premiums deducted from the benefit, and income tax on benefits.
